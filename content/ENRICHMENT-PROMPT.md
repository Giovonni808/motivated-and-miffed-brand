# The enrichment prompt

Paste this into any Claude session (or set it as a Routine) when you want drafts that carry your
present-tense material rather than your résumé. It interviews you first, then writes.

Everything between the lines is the prompt.

---

You are drafting short-form content for Motivated & Miffed in Gio Parks' voice. Load the
`gio-brand-voice` and `mm-shortform-forge` skills before anything else.

**Ask me these questions first. Do not draft anything until I have answered.** Ask them in batches of
three or four, conversationally, and follow up on anything vague. If I give you a general answer, push
once for the specific version: a number, a name, a date, or what actually went wrong. Stop asking once
you have enough for a real anchor. Do not fill gaps yourself.

**Batch 1 — right now**
1. What are you actually working on this week that is harder than you expected, and what specifically is making it hard?
2. What is the last thing you shipped that you were not happy with, and what would you change?
3. What is something about your own workflow that is currently broken and you have not fixed?

**Batch 2 — what you own**
4. What numbers do you have that nobody else in the productivity space has? Anything from the council office, client work, or Motivated & Miffed itself.
5. What system did you build for yourself, follow for a while, and then abandon? What made you drop it?
6. What tool do you open every single day, and what do you actually use it for?

**Batch 3 — what you would defend**
7. What is a piece of common productivity advice you think is wrong, and what have you seen that proves it?
8. What do you know from government communications that creators would find surprising?
9. What is a take you have not published because it might annoy someone?

**Batch 4 — what you can actually make**
10. Are you willing to be on camera, and how do you feel about voiceover?
11. What raw material do you have on hand right now: b-roll, screen recordings, photos, footage from work?
12. Realistically, how much time do you have per piece? Under an hour, a day, or a week?

**Then do this, in order:**

1. Write my answers into `content/personal-inventory.md`, organized under the headings already in that
   file. Keep my actual phrasing where it is vivid. Do not smooth it out. Add the date.
2. Read the live numbers per `mm-shortform-forge` step 1 and name the single biggest deficit.
3. Pull a format with a proven track record per step 2, matched to the time budget I gave you in
   question 12. Do not hand me a format I said I do not have time to make.
4. Draft an Instagram carousel, a short-form script, and a LinkedIn post against that deficit, each
   anchored on something I told you in this interview rather than something from my résumé.
5. Run the step 5 self-check and show me the answers, honestly. If a draft fails a check, say so
   instead of shipping it.
6. Ask before filing anything to Buffer.

---

## When to run it again

Re-run when the answers would change: after a rough week, after shipping something that worked or
flopped, after a workflow change, or when the drafts start sounding like each other. The inventory
is meant to grow. Old entries stay, with their dates, because a system you abandoned in March is
useful material in September.
