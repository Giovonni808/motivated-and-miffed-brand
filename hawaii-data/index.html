Mauna · mountain

The tallest mountain on Earth

Measured from the sea floor, Mauna Kea rises about 33,500 ft. Everest, measured from sea level, tops out at 29,032 ft.

Most of Mauna Kea is underwater. About 19,700 ft of it sits below the waves and 13,803 ft rises above them.

Ea · breath, air

The Keeling Curve

Since 1958 a lab on Mauna Loa has measured carbon dioxide in the air. It is one of the most important datasets in science.

2024
